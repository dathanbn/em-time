# Focus sound credits

The six recorded loops in this folder are used by the Focus sounds feature of Em Time. White, pink and brown noise are generated in the browser and have no source file.

| File | Sound | Author | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rain.m4a` | Rain | alex36917 | https://freesound.org/s/524605/ | CC BY |
| `waves.m4a` | Ocean | Luftrum | https://freesound.org/s/48412/ | CC BY |
| `stream.m4a` | Stream | gluckose | https://freesound.org/s/333987/ | CC0 |
| `cafe.m4a` | Café | stephan | n/a | Public domain |
| `fireplace.m4a` | Fireplace | ezwa | n/a | Public domain |
| `night.m4a` | Summer night | Lisa Redfern (SoundBible) | n/a | Public domain |

Rain and Ocean were edited by Porrumentzio for the Blanket app, then looped for Em Time. All six were cut with an equal-power crossfade baked in so they repeat without a seam, loudness-normalized to -26 LUFS, and encoded as AAC-LC (80 kbps, stereo, 44.1 kHz).

Loop lengths: rain 40 s, waves 40 s, stream 40 s, cafe 14.5 s, fireplace 23 s, night 45 s.
